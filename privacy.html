SkynetApp privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

What the app accesses

With your permission, SkynetApp uses the Google drive.file scope. It can only see and change Google Sheets files that the app itself created. It cannot see your other files, your email or your contacts.

How data is used and stored

  • Spreadsheet content is read or written only when the person running the app asks for it.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored encrypted on the app owner's own server. It is not shared with anyone.
  • Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not transferred to third parties.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
